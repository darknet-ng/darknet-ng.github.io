FAQ

Questions, answered.

Here are a few common questions about Bright Horizons Collective and getting involved.

Who can volunteer?

Anyone who wants to help is welcome. Some activities may have age, safety, or training requirements, and we share those details before each event.

How are donations used?

Donations support community projects, event supplies, family resources, and the basic coordination needed to deliver them responsibly.

Can my organization partner with you?

Yes. We welcome partnerships with neighborhood groups, schools, businesses, and fellow nonprofits whose goals align with our mission.

How can I hear about future events?

Follow our community updates and sign up for event announcements when registration opens.

Do you accept in-kind donations?

We do when items match a current need. Please contact us before donating goods so we can direct them where they will be most useful.
