About us

Community is our greatest resource.

Darknet-NG, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to making cybersecurity education engaging, accessible, and community-driven. Through narrative-based Capture the Flag (CTF) events, Darknet-NG gives participants hands-on opportunities to explore a range of cybersecurity topics, solve practical challenges, and build skills alongside fellow learners in an inclusive, collaborative environment.

What guides our work

Education

Teaching computer security for players to take back to their community to help secure everyone

Gamification

Learning about some security topics can be boring or so vast it is hard to know where to start. Turning it into a game makes it more interesting and provide paths to learn.

Story

Book learning and quizes are forgettable. Moments of coming together on a puzzle or finally solving that one problem make it memorable.
